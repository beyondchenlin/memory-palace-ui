How Memory Palace works

  • Warm cartoon HUD with clear meters — easy to read while you type
  • No captions over the video — assist text sits above the typing rails
  • Bottom reserves space for the system keyboard — no fake keys
  • Interpret opens on the clip (audio ducks); Palace is your saved review
  • Today's tone colors your session · optional daily auto-tone
  • Learn from film packs or your own uploads
  • Desktop & Pad show a device shell · phone opens full-screen
  • Each tone has a mood pet — tap it on desktop to change tone
